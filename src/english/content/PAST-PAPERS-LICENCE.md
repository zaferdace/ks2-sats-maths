# Past-paper questions: licence and attribution

The questions in `gps-items-past-papers.json` are taken from the Standards and Testing Agency's
key stage 2 English grammar, punctuation and spelling tests (Paper 1: questions) for the years
2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Each item records its test year and
question number in its `source` field. Wording has been adapted only where the app's input
(tapping or typing) differs from the paper (for example "Circle" becomes "Tap").
